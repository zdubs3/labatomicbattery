Containment & safety

Safe.

This is the containment design and the regulatory path it's built around — not a finished, certified product. Every core — Type Creation, Eternal Cell, or inside a Mozez Lion Type E — is engineered to the same containment spec, and follows the same regime that already governs industrial gauges and medical sealed sources.

🔒

Hermetic titanium shell (design spec)

The design calls for a laser-welded Ti-6Al-4V (aerospace-grade titanium) can, engineered to a helium leak-tight spec of ≤1×10⁻⁹ mbar·L/s. Every unit that reaches production is planned to be wipe-tested and dose-surveyed before it leaves the facility — that qualification hasn't happened yet.

🛡️

Shielding sized to the isotope

Type Creation units are designed with a low-Z bremsstrahlung shield for its beta emission; Eternal Cell units with a gamma shield sized to keep external dose at or below the facility limit. Not a one-size-fits-all shield.

⚖

Built for independent classification

Every core is designed to the ISO 2919 sealed-source standard — independently rated for temperature, external pressure, impact, vibration, and puncture resistance. Formal classification happens during licensing, before any unit ships.

✅

Never handled raw, by design

The plan sources the isotope pre-encapsulated and leak-tested from an already-licensed supplier, so nobody on the assembly line would ever open, machine, or handle bare radioactive material.

ISO 2919 — sealed source classification 10 CFR Part 30/32 — NRC byproduct material license 10 CFR 71 / 49 CFR 173 — radioactive transport UL 1642 / 2580 — battery safety

These are the design targets and the licensing framework this program is built for — not certifications this product already holds. Nothing ships until that licensing is complete.

How it works

Decay in, electricity out.

Every unit runs the same three-step chain — only step three changes depending on which product you pick.

1

Decay

The isotope core emits radiation continuously — no chain reaction, no fuel to add, no way to turn it off.

2

Convert

Decay energy crosses a betavoltaic or thermophotovoltaic junction and becomes electric current.

3

Deliver (or buffer)

Type Creation and Eternal Cell units deliver current directly. Mozez Lion Type E units trickle-charge a chemical buffer that discharges in fast, kW-class bursts.

Is this actually radioactive?

Yes. The Type Creation and Eternal Cell cores are both built on real, commercially licensable isotopes already used in industrial gauges and radioisotope generators. The design calls for a hermetic, laser-welded titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) can, helium leak-tested and built to the ISO 2919 sealed-source standard, with shielding sized for the isotope inside — see containment & safety for the full rundown and what's still design-stage versus certified. Any unit that reaches a customer will be regulated and licensed accordingly. Reaching out does not put a radioactive device in your hands today.

What's the difference between the three, really?

Type Creation and Eternal Cell are the same converter technology running a different isotope — Type Creation trades service life for real wattage, Eternal Cell trades wattage for extraordinary service life. The Mozez Lion Type E takes either core and adds a chemical buffer, so it can also answer short bursts of much higher power than the isotope alone could ever deliver.
