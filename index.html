Safety

🔒

Sealed shut, for good

Every unit is designed to be fully sealed before it's ever built — nothing to open, refill, or maintain, and no way for what's inside to get out.

🛡️

Shielded by design

Each core is designed with shielding sized to keep it safe to be near in everyday use. Not a one-size-fits-all shield — it's matched to the option.

⚖

Built for independent testing

The design targets independent safety testing and classification before any unit ships — not just a claim on a spec sheet.

✅

Never handled raw, by design

The plan sources the atomic powered material already sealed from an already-licensed supplier, so nobody on the assembly line would ever open or handle it directly.

How it works

Atomic power in, electricity out.

Every unit runs the same three-step chain — only step three changes depending on which product you pick.

1

Generate

Atomic powered material continuously generates energy — no chain reaction, no fuel to add, no way to turn it off.

2

Convert

That energy becomes usable electric current, ready to power whatever it's connected to.

3

Deliver (or boost)

Cub and Lion units deliver that current directly. Type E units also keep a reserve charged and ready, for fast bursts of extra power when you need them.

Before you reach out

What to know before you contact us.

This is a real early-stage engineering program, not a shipping product. We'd rather you know that going in.

How much does it cost?

We don't publish pricing on this page. The atomic powered material and the licensing it requires dominate cost, not assembly, and both scale with the option and quantity. Email us and we'll work through real numbers for what you have in mind.

Is this actually radioactive?

Yes. Every option uses real atomic powered material, sealed and shielded by design — see safety for the full rundown and what's still design-stage versus certified. Any unit that reaches a customer will be regulated and licensed accordingly. Reaching out does not put a device in your hands today.

What's the difference between the three, really?

Cub and Lion use the same atomic powered material in different amounts — Cub trades lifespan for more power right now, Lion trades power for an extraordinary lifespan. Type E takes either core — sold as Cub Type E or Lion Type E — and adds a boost, so it can also answer short bursts of much higher power than the core alone could ever deliver.
